About Eduford

A single university,
built across three cities.

For over a century, Eduford has trained economists, engineers and artists who go on to lead across every industry and continent. Our students take courses that move between campuses, working alongside faculty who split their careers between the classroom and the field.

We're accredited by the International Higher Education Council and rank among the top research institutions on three continents — recognition that follows from the work our graduates do, not the other way around.
